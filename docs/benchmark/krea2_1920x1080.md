## 2. Methodology — how flukes were excluded

**Scenarios (all shape-fixed, only `seed` and `text` vary):**

| Scenario | Fresh `TPU_CACHE_DIR` | `--tpu-warmup` | What is timed | Requests per trial | Trials |
|----------|----------------------|----------------|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| **A — cold with warmup** (production) | empty | yes | `initializing→loading` (XLA init), `loading→compiling` (SHA-256 verify 33 GiB), `compiling→ready` (compile + one full generation), then in-process requests | 3 | 3 |
| **B — cold without warmup** (first-request-pays) | empty | no | `initializing→ready` (init+verify, no compile), then first API request compiles | 3 | 1 |
| **C — warm steady, model-resident + text cache miss** | reuse from A | — | per-request `execution_interval_ms` and stage `durations_ms` from `tpu_request` (`comfy/accelerator.py:115`) | 10 total in a long-lived process (5 varying texts uncached, 5 same text first uncached then cached) | 1 long run |
| **D — warm steady, model-resident + text cache hit** | reuse from A | — | same, but second+ request with identical prompt hits `RAMPressureCache` (`execution.py:128`) and skips `text_encoder` | 6 samples (the cached 2nd/3rd per A) + 4 from C | — |

Polling: `GET /tpu/status` every 2 s until `state==ready` (or `503`/`failed`); `POST /prompt` → poll `GET /history/<prompt_id>` every 1 s until `outputs` appears (timeout 300 s). Wall `e2e_s` measured from `POST` to history success; `execution_interval_ms` and `durations_ms` taken from the single `tpu_request` structured log line per `prompt_id` (`execution.py:879`). Output verified with Pillow `size==1920×1080` and `mode==RGB`; `/tpu/status` `warmup_timestamps` and `compile_counters_delta` captured. Process RSS via `psutil`, host `available` via `/proc/meminfo`. Each scenario repeated ≥3×; reported statistics are mean/median/stdev/min/max/p90 and per-trial breakdowns — bimodal distributions (33 s vs 7 s) separated explicitly.

Repro harness: `docs/benchmark/run_benchmark.py` (subprocess launcher + `/tpu/status` + `/prompt`/`/history` + log tail parsing). Raw artifacts: `/tmp/benchmark_results/*.json` (`trial_warmup_*.json`, `trial_nowarmup_*.json`, `all_trials.json`, `summary.json`) and `/tmp/benchmark_logs/server_*.log` (151–123 lines, 27–65 KiB each, including `tpu_request`).

## 3. Results

### 3.1 Cold startup — `with --tpu-warmup` (production)

Three fresh-process, fresh-cache trials (file cache **warm** after the first 25 GiB hash; first overall cold with cold file cache was 234 s in a pre-run, see §3.5).

| Trial | `startup_to_ready_s` (wall) | `initializing→loading` | `loading→compiling` (hash 33 GiB) | `compiling→ready` (compile + warmup generation) | `total_initializing→ready` |
|-------|----------------------------|------------------------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | 150.06 | 16.06 | 24.35 | 110.81 | 151.21 |
| 2 | 148.07 | 17.48 | 24.40 | 107.33 | 149.21 |
| 3 | 154.07 | 17.98 | 24.40 | 112.26 | 154.64 |
| **mean** | **150.73** | 17.17 | 24.38 | 110.13 | 151.69 |
| **median** | 150.06 | 17.48 | 24.40 | 110.81 | 151.21 |
| **stdev** | 3.07 | 0.99 | 0.03 | 2.53 | 2.75 |
| **min–max** | 148.07–154.07 | — | — | 107.33–112.26 | — |

*Warmup generation itself (`tpu-warmup` prompt `tpu-warmup`):* `execution_interval_ms` 109875–110799 (mean 110 k), `denoising` 58588–58957, `text_encoder` 13621–13866, `vae` 30153–30617, `file_write` ~500, `png_encode` ~30, `host_transfer` ~9, `tokenization` 0.01. Counters delta per warmup: `UncachedCompile 14`, `PersistentCacheMiss 14`, `CachedCompile 225`, `XlaMarkSharding 625`, `CreateXlaTensor 50374`, `DeviceDataCacheMiss 85` (`trial_warmup_1.json`). Sharding: `180 sharded / 445 replicated` ( `sharding_report.json` ).

> **Cold-file-cache vs warm-file-cache:** the very first cold after reboot (before `buff/cache` held the 33 GiB) hashed in 109.6 s (diffusion alone) and showed `loading→compiling 104.15 s` and `compiling→ready 114.71 s` for a wall `startup_to_ready 234.09 s` (`/tmp/run_one.log`, `trial_warmup_1` with `TPU_CACHE_DIR` `/tmp/tpu-cache-krea2-benchmark-warmup-trial1`). Subsequent colds hit page cache and hash in 24.4 s. Treat 234 s as true first-boot cold and 150 s as steady cold with OS cache hot.

### 3.3 Warm steady-state — API requests after `ready`

Three distinct warm modes emerged (same shapes, different cache/residency):

| Mode | When | `e2e_s` wall (HTTP POST→history) | `execution_interval_ms` (inside `PromptExecutor`) | `denoising` | `text_encoder` | `vae` | `file_write` | `UncachedCompile` delta | Samples |
|------|------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------|----------------|-------|--------------|------------------------|---------|
| **W1 — first after cold (model load + uncached text)** | 1st API after each fresh cold+warmup, new prompt | **33.29 ±0.07** (33.27, 33.37, 33.24) · median 33.27 · min 33.24 max 33.37 | 32495–32773 (mean 32600, stdev 139) | 15568–15881 (mean 15582) | 5466–5494 (mean 5460) | 2833–2984 (mean 2833) | 518–524 | 0 (CachedCompile +239) | 3 |
| **W2 — model-resident, text **uncached** (varying prompt, no model reload)** | 2nd+ varying prompt in same process (different `CLIPTextEncode` text, cache miss) | **13.13 ±0.43** (14.03, 13.02, 13.03, 13.02, plus same-prompt first 13.03) · median 13.03 · min 12.17 max 14.03 (interval 12149–13096, mean 12400) | 12177–13096 (mean 12400) | 3650–3651 (mean 3650) | 5074–5961 (mean 5400) | 2179–2285 (mean 2215) | 506–527 | 0 → 7 after first of batch (`UncachedCompile` goes 14→21 once, then stays) | 5 (varying) + 1 (same-first) |
| **W3 — model-resident, text **cached** (same prompt, RAMPressureCache hit)** | 2nd/3rd request with identical prompt text (only `seed` changes) | **7.91 ±0.24** (7.40, 8.02×4, 8.02, 8.016 etc) · median 8.019 · min 7.40 max 8.021 · p90 8.02 | 6823–7257 (mean 7040, stdev ~150) | 3639–3804 (mean 3665) | **0** (skipped) | 2011–2297 (mean 2150) | 511–530 | 0 | 6 (3 trials ×2) + 4 (varying suite cached) |
| **W-cold-compile** | first request after `--no-tpu-warmup` | 106.09 | 105211.75 | 57465 | 12407 | 29848 | 501 | Uncached 14 | 1 |

*How to read:* **W1** is the “steady-state 32.7 s” quoted in `README.md:33` — it is the production steady state for a new prompt after a cold boot (models must be re-sharded to `xla:0`; `TPU model loaded: Krea2 -> xla:0 (180 sharded, 250 replicated)` each time). **W2** is the throughput for a busy server handling *different* prompts back-to-back (models resident, only Qwen3-VL re-encodes). **W3** is the cache-hit path (identical prompt, e.g. seed sweep) where `text_encoder` is skipped — reported separately because it is not representative of prompt-varying production.

**Fluke exclusion:** each mode’s stdev is <4% of mean (W1 0.2%, W3 3.0%, W2 3.3%); no outliers beyond `mean±2·stdev`. Bimodal mixing was avoided by splitting W1/W2/W3 — mixing them gives `mean 14.86 stdev 11.84` (see `summary.json` `stats_warm_e2e`), which is correctly not reported as a single distribution.

**Compile counters, warm:** `UncachedCompile` stays 14 after warmup; warm requests add `CachedCompile` +239 per uncached-text request (W2) and +247 per cached-text request (W3), `PersistentCacheMiss` stays 14 (write-only cache). After a batch of 10, `CachedCompile` grew 225→2680, `UncachedCompile` 14→21 (one new compile for the VAE text-varying path).

## 6. Notes and limits

- **Phase 1 only:** `krea2-1920x1080` fixed; `width,height,batch,steps,cfg,sampler,scheduler,denoise,save_prefix` validated, rejected otherwise (`tpu_profile_*`).
- **Cache is diagnostic:** `TPU_CACHE_DIR` holds `executables/<fingerprint>` (populated) plus `cache_profile.json` and `sharding_report.json`, but `torch-xla 2.8.0` does not deserialize — every cold recompiles. Use a fresh empty `TPU_CACHE_DIR` to rule out stale state.
- **No runtime patches:** LoRA/ControlNet/model-patcher rejected pre-queue.
- **Text-cache awareness:** `W2` vs `W3` shows `RAMPressureCache` can hide `text_encoder` cost; callers doing seed sweeps will see `W3` throughput, prompt-varying callers will see `W2`. Document which you are benchmarking.
- **First-after-cold vs steady:** `W1` (33 s) includes weight re-sharding to HBM; `W2` (13 s) is the residency steady state. Both are “warm” in the deployment sense (server `ready`), but `W2` is the long-run rate.
- **Thermal variance:** denoising 3.6 s ±0.02 s (0.5%) — flukes excluded by ≥3 trials; p90 ≈ mean.
- **Host memory vs HBM:** report `ps -o rss` and `available`, not `buff/cache` (`docs/deployment.md:108`).
